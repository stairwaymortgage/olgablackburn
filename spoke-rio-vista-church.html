Olga Blackburn · Fort Lauderdale Community

More Than a Sunday Service

When Jim and I settled in Fort Lauderdale, one of the first things we did was find a church home. We weren't looking for a performance. We were looking for real people doing real life together — and that's what we found at Rio Vista Church.

The Bible study group has been especially meaningful to me. As a Czech woman living in America, I sometimes felt like an outsider in faith spaces — the culture was unfamiliar, the expressions were different. Rio Vista made room for all of that. For my questions. For my accent. For my honest wrestling with Scripture.

"Church is where I go to be reminded that I am not the center of my own story — and that's the most freeing thing in the world."

If you're in Fort Lauderdale and looking for a church community — especially if you're new to the area — I'd love to introduce you. Come to a service, come to Bible study, or just send me a message. Community is too important to navigate alone.

Rio Vista Church is located in Fort Lauderdale's Rio Vista neighborhood — beautiful, accessible, and as warm as the city itself.
